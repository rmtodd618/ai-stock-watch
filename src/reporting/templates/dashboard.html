What this is. Every weekday at 22:00 UTC the tool scores the watchlist and writes a snapshot to S3 recording what it said and the rules that produced it. This page reads those snapshots and joins them to the prices that actually followed. Nothing here is reconstructed after the fact — these are rows the tool committed to before the outcome was known.

What it is not. Not a prediction, not advice, and not a claim that the strategy works. The honest summary from 4.7 years of backtesting is that its information ratio against simply holding the same names was 0.03, with 23% annualised tracking error. This page exists to find out whether that holds up, and the only way to find out is to wait.
